SUPPORT

Need help or found a bug? Email manbem13@myyahoo.com and include your device model.

How do I steer?

Tap the left half of the screen to move one lane left, the right half to move right.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top right. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app, get a call or lock your phone. Tap Resume for a 3-2-1 countdown, or End run to bank your score.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app resets your progress.

No sound?

Check Sound and Music in the menu or pause screen, and your device volume.
