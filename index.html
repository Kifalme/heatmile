HEAT MILE

One thumb. Endless neon coast road. How far can you go?

Tap left or right to change lanes. Pass close to traffic for combos, thread the needle, grab nitro and gold briefcases, and drive through the gold gate at your best distance. Daily missions, a garage of five cars, and a pause button whenever you need it.

For iPhone and iPad. No accounts. Free, with an occasional ad between runs.
