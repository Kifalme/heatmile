HEAT MILE

One thumb. Endless neon coast road. How far can you go?

Tap left or right to change lanes on an endless neon road that keeps changing: Sunset Strip, the Neon Tunnel, the Rain District, the Harbor Bridge and Downtown Lights. Chain near-miss combos, grab nitro, magnets, shields and double points, pick from five cars that each drive differently, and race the Daily Run on the Game Center leaderboards. Levels, 21 trophies, missions and a pause button whenever you need it.

For iPhone and iPad. No accounts. Free, with an occasional ad between runs.
