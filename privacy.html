PRIVACY

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version: Heat Mile collects nothing. No ads, no tracking, no accounts, and the game never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your best score, cash, cars, missions, streak, records and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we collect

Nothing. Heat Mile has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code, and does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
