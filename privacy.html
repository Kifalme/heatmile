PRIVACY

Last updated November 17, 2026 (ads added)

The short version: your game progress stays on your device, there are no accounts, and Heat Mile shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob between some runs. Those ads are the only part of the game that uses the internet.

What stays on your device

Your best score, cash, cars, missions, streak, records and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Heat Mile shows a full-screen ad after some finished runs, never in the middle of a run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, a general location estimate, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Heat Mile asks your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still get ads, just less personalized ones, and the game works the same. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the UK and European Economic Area, Google also asks for your consent before showing ads.

What we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing ourselves: no accounts, no analytics, no location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob uses for ads, described above.

Children

Heat Mile is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
